# Pricing — AgentFlow Pro for Dealerships

Two-part pricing: a one-time setup fee to integrate and configure the agent stack against
a specific dealership's CRM/DMS, and a monthly subscription for the running agents and
the branded Dealership App.

## Setup fee: $5,000 – $10,000 (one-time)

Covers CRM/DMS integration, agent configuration for the dealership's inventory/lender/
review-platform accounts, brand setup for the Dealership App, and a guided rollout with
the sales and F&I team.

| Scope | Fee |
|---|---|
| Single rooftop, standard CRM/DMS (one of the major platforms), Starter or Growth tier | **$5,000** |
| Single rooftop, non-standard/legacy DMS or multiple data sources to reconcile | **$6,500 – $7,500** |
| Single rooftop, Pro tier with full white-label app (custom domain, custom brand system) | **$7,500 – $8,500** |
| Multi-rooftop / dealer group rollout | **$8,500 – $10,000** (scales with rooftop count) |

Setup fee is credited toward the first three months of the subscription for annual-
contract customers.

## Why this structure

- **Starter** exists to get a dealership live fast on the modules with the clearest,
  fastest ROI (content/lead capture, review generation, reactivating dead CRM leads) at a
  price a single-rooftop independent can approve without a committee.
- **Growth** is the tier most dealerships land on: it adds the operational modules
  (inventory/pricing discipline, trade-in appraisal, service retention) that turn this
  from a marketing tool into a daily-use ops system.
- **Pro** adds the highest-stakes, highest-oversight module (Finance & Deal Desk, which
  touches lender submissions and F&I paperwork) plus real-time escalation and full
  white-label branding — the tier built for a dealership that wants the whole floor
  running on it, or a group standardizing across rooftops.

## What's not included

Ad spend, social platform fees, lender network fees, CRM/DMS licensing, and review
platform costs are the dealership's own accounts and costs — AgentFlow Pro operates
those accounts on the dealership's behalf, it doesn't replace or resell them.
